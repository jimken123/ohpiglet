This was one of the biggest hikes of the entire summer. We hiked into our favourite orange box refugi - Refugi Mataró Gerber - and stayed the night. Papa, Boí, Saoirse, and Aoife.

![yo yo]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/images/2026/IMG_20260829_171328.jpg) 

In total, the first day is about a two-hour hike. We headed up in the afternoon, to have time to cook dinner before dark.

![yo yo]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/images/2026/IMG_20260829_174047.jpg) 

Late afternoon sun on the lake.

![yo yo]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/images/2026/IMG_20260829_180431.jpg) 

This trail is really well signposted.

![yo yo]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/images/2026/IMG_20260829_183937.jpg) 

We finally see our destination, the orange box tied to the top of the giant rock. Boí led the way on the final stretch, waiting for us just before the final push to the top. He's a real mountain guide.

![yo yo]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/images/2026/IMG_20260829_191328.jpg) 

Cooking our newly invented favourite camping dish - sopita de caldo with chopped-up frankfurts.

![yo yo]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/images/2026/IMG_20260829_200245.jpg) 

Sunset is upon us just after dinner and this is the view across towards Espot skistation.

![yo yo]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/images/2026/IMG_20260829_203512.jpg) 

The island in the lake just below our refugi.

![yo yo]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/images/2026/IMG_20260829_203610.jpg)

![yo yo]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/images/2026/IMG_20260829_210725.jpg)

![yo yo]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/images/2026/IMG_20260830_073210.jpg)
